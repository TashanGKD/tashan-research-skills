# scientific-structure-reviewer
## 一句话定位
[来源] Review a scientific manuscript outline or draft for peer-review-ready structure, scientific story, contribution framing, section order, paper-type fit, motivation, gap, and narrative coherence. Use before detailed technical or prose review. Do not use for cita
