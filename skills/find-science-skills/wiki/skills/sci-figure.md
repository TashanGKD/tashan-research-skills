# sci-figure
## 一句话定位
[来源] Extracts figures and sub-figures from academic PDF papers. Supports Fig/Figure, Scheme, Chart, Supplementary Figure, Extended Data Figure (Nature), and Chinese equivalents (图/方案/示意图/附图/补充图). Sub-figure label recognition supports (a)/(A)/a)/(i)/(1)/a. formats.
