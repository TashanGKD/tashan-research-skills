# experiment-design
## 一句话定位
[来源] Design experiment plans with progressive stages — initial implementation, baseline tuning, creative research, and ablation studies. Plan baselines, datasets, hyperparameter sweeps, and evaluation metrics. Use when planning experiments for a research paper.
