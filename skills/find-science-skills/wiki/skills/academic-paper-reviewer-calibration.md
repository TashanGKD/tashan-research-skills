# academic-paper-reviewer-calibration
## 一句话定位
[来源] Reviewer calibration workflow for comparing review behavior against a rubric, benchmark set, or expected reviewer posture. Use when the user wants to understand review reliability or scoring consistency rather than critique one paper only.
